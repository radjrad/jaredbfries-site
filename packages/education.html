What the platforms do that I don't build

A parent app with photos and daily reports · a tap-to-sign-in kiosk · in-app card and bank payments · subsidy billing. If those matter to your school, keep them. I'll build around them.

What I build that no platform sells

A calendar system your team actually uses · an inbox that sorts itself · a binder so your directors run everything · training at each site · your data in your own Drive, exportable any time · no per-child creep, no renewal call, no cancellation phone tree.

FAQ

We already use Brightwheel (or Procare, or…).

Keep it if it's working. Most schools I meet use a platform for the parent app and still run invoicing, the waitlist, licensing sheets, and the calendar by hand. I build the parts that are still manual, around whatever you already have. The Walkthrough is where we decide which is which.

What do we need to have?

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, a spreadsheet you're willing to make the truth, and about an hour a week from you while we build.

Will my directors actually use it?

That's the whole design. We build it with them, in tools they already know. Nothing launches until a director has run it start to finish without me in the room.

What happens when something breaks?

Thirty days of fixes are included with every build. After that, the binder covers most of it, and Systems Care is there if you want a standing hand.

Do you work outside the Bay Area?

Yes. The Walkthrough is best in person; the builds work remotely. I've also done the Walkthrough as two half-days on video with a director holding the camera.

Why fixed prices?

Because you should know the number before you say yes, and because I've built these enough times to carry the risk.

Tell me about your mess.

Bring the invoice that takes all afternoon, the waitlist you're not sure is complete, or the part of the week that always feels harder than it should. We'll find a calmer way through it.
