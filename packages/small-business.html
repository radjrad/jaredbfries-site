Runs without you.

For owners who want a business that works when they step away, and a team that can run the day without routing everything through them.

Most small businesses already have an operating system. It's the owner. Every price, every process, every "here's how we handle that" lives in one head. That works right up until you want a real vacation, a second location, a bigger team, or a buyer.

I move the operating system out of your head and into things your team can run: one source of truth for clients, jobs, and billing, invoicing that sends itself, a binder for every job that repeats, an onboarding kit so the next hire is productive in days instead of months, and a dashboard that tells you how the business is doing without anyone having to ask you. You own all of it. Nothing sits in my account, nothing needs a new subscription, and the binder means the next hire can run it without calling me.

Same way I work with schools: in the tools you already pay for, with the people who'll use it, documented so it outlasts me.

FAQ

What kinds of businesses?

Service businesses where the owner is still the operating system: practices, studios, shops, agencies, brokerages, trades. If your customers are families and your team still leans on you for how things get done, you're squarely who this is for.

We're not selling. Is this still for us?

Yes. Most owners I work with aren't. They want a vacation that's actually a vacation, or a manager who can run the place without a hundred texts.

My team isn't that small. Is this still for us?

Yes. Size isn't the test; routing is. If a team of five or fifty still comes to you for pricing, process, and "how do we handle this," the work is the same: move it out of your head and into systems the team can run.

What tools do you build in?

Whatever you already pay for: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, your existing invoicing or point-of-sale, your website. I'm not going to hand you a new subscription.

How does pricing work?

Every build is fixed price and quoted before we start, usually after the first day on site or an intro call. The first day is credited toward the build that follows. There are no per-seat or per-location fees.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything lives in your Google account, your Sheets, your tools. I don't hold logins, host anything, or charge a monthly fee to keep it running. At handoff you get the binder, a walkthrough with whoever runs it day to day, and 30 days of fixes if something breaks.
