About the founder

Operator. Educator. System builder.

Jared B. Fries has spent 15+ years building the systems that help organizations grow — across classrooms, school leadership, district administration, and small business operations.

His approach pairs the human side of change with the discipline of durable systems, shaped by an M.Ed. from Harvard University and a B.A. from the University of Pennsylvania.

He brings that full-table perspective to small business owners facing operational complexity, process friction, and growth that can no longer run on heroic effort.

Summers, you'll find him working the gates at Stern Grove Festival, helping make live music free and open to all of San Francisco.
