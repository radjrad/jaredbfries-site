What I do for schools

Rosters, records & billing

One roster that everything else reads from: invoicing, sign-in sheets, contact groups, and the dashboard. Monthly billing goes from start to sent in minutes, siblings combined, PDFs filed, and a log of every send.

Licensing & compliance

Sign-in sheets and site records generated from the roster, one page per class, with the licensing rule printed on the sheet. The knock becomes a non-event.

Enrollment & the waitlist

Every application your school has ever received in one tracker. A waitlist you can pull from by age and start date. An application families actually finish, where returning families skip the fee.

Calendars, inbox & family communication

Calendars that tell the right people the right things. An inbox that sorts itself. Contact groups that update themselves, and templates in your voice for every stage from "we got your application" to "here's your spot."

Training & the binder

A printable how-to for every system, short video walkthroughs, and live sessions with your directors at each site. Nothing launches until a director has run it start to finish without me in the room.

New sites & practical automation

A milestone calendar from licensing to rent day, a "Coming Soon" launch to your wait pool, and the small automations that take the repetitive work off your directors' plates.

FAQ

We already use Brightwheel (or Procare, or…).

Keep it if it's working. I build the parts that are still manual, around whatever you already have. The Walkthrough is where we decide which is which.

What do we need to have?

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, a spreadsheet you're willing to make the truth, and about an hour a week from you while we build.

Will my directors actually use it?

That's the whole design. We build it with them, in tools they already know. Nothing launches until a director has run it start to finish without me in the room.

How does pricing work?

Every build is fixed price and quoted before we start, usually after the Walkthrough or an intro call. There are no per-child or per-site fees, and the Walkthrough is credited toward any build that follows.

What happens when something breaks?

Thirty days of fixes are included with every build. After that, the binder covers most of it, and Systems Care, an optional light monthly retainer, is there if you want a standing hand.

Do you work outside the Bay Area?

Yes. The Walkthrough is best in person; the builds work remotely. I've also done the Walkthrough as two half-days on video with a director holding the camera.

Tell me about your mess.

Bring the invoice that takes all afternoon, the waitlist you're not sure is complete, or the part of the week that always feels harder than it should. We'll find a calmer way through it.
