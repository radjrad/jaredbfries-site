Featured project

Tiny Giants Schools: three sites, one founder, and a wait pool nobody could see.

When I met Jen, she was running two preschools and opening a third, and every system in the building ran through her. Invoices went out one family at a time. Applications lived in three documents from two different eras, and nobody could say how many families were waiting. Mornings started with ninety minutes of inbox. The sign-in sheets wouldn't have survived a licensing visit.

We built the fix together, in the Google Workspace she already paid for. One roster that's true, feeding invoicing, sign-in sheets, and contact groups. One enrollment tracker holding every application the school has ever received, with a waitlist she can pull from by age and start date. An application where returning families skip the fee. Six calendars that tell the right people the right things. An inbox that sorts itself. A "Coming Soon" launch for the third site. And a how-to binder, so her directors run all of it without me.
