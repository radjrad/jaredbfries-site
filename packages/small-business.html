FAQ

What kinds of businesses?

Service businesses where the owner is still the operating system: practices, studios, shops, agencies, trades. If your customers are families and your team is small, you're squarely who this is for.

We're not selling. Is this still for us?

Yes. Most owners I work with aren't. They want a vacation that's actually a vacation, or a manager who can run the place without a hundred texts.

What tools do you build in?

Whatever you already pay for: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, your existing invoicing or point-of-sale, your website. I'm not going to hand you a new subscription.
